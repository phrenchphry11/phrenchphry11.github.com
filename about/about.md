I'm Holly, a software engineering manager based in San Francisco. I currently lead two engineering teams at <a href="https://www.fieldwire.com">Fieldwire by Hilti</a>: one's a full-stack engineering team, and one's a bit more computer vision focused, which has been very fun to learn more about.

Before Fieldwire, I spent several years as an engineering manager on Redfin's Tour Automation team, and before that as a senior developer at Alloy, where we helped improve data systems in the progressive political campaign space. (Also - I was a Redfin boomering, along with working at a few other great startups along the way.)

These days I spend most of my time on the management side: hiring, growing engineers into their next roles, and leading with empathy to build better teams while the tech world is changing so rapidly.

While I'm not at work, I love reading, playing Bridge (often at <a href="http://www.bridgebase.com">Bridge Base Online</a>), watching the <a href="http://www.sfballet.org/">San Francisco Ballet</a>, cycling (including the occasional multi-day cycling trip), and exploring San Francisco's neighborhoods. I also enjoy travel, a love which developed during my college study abroad program in Budapest, Hungary in college, where I later returned to work at <a href="http://www.prezi.com">Prezi</a> for about a year.
